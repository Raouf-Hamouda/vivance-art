The gallery

Vivance Art is a Paris gallery dedicated to the exhibition and dissemination of Latin American art.

Our mission is to bring the rich cultural heritage and creative diversity of Latin American artists to international stages, and to open a vibrant dialogue between their expressions and a global audience. Specialising in contemporary art, Vivance champions emerging and established artists who challenge the boundaries of artistic language and convention.
