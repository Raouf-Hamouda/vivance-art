Exhibition · Latin America and Caribbean Weeks 2025

Origins

28 May 2025 to 8 Jun 2025 · 8 pm to midnight
Vivance Art Gallery, 10 avenue de Corbera, Paris 12e

As part of the Latin America and Caribbean Weeks 2025 in Paris, Vivance Art presents Tuty Navarro as its central artist. Known as Synesthesia, Navarro blends audiovisual art, music, technology and spirituality. At SALC2025 she introduces Ancestral Origins, an immersive audiovisual installation inspired by Yagé: a journey through the roots, identity and cosmovision of Indigenous peoples, using fractals, videomapping and soundscapes.
