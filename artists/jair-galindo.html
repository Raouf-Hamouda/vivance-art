Jair Galindo

Born Barranquilla, ColombiaLives and works in Barranquilla, Colombia

Jair Galindo is a Colombian visual artist and educator whose practice is centered on drawing. He studied at the Faculty of Fine Arts of the Universidad del Atlántico in Barranquilla. His graduation project was selected for the XIII Regional Salon of Caribbean Artists and received the university’s highest academic distinction.

Galindo has developed a distinctive drawing process in which the conventional pencil is replaced by a precision knife or scalpel. By cutting and removing material from the surface, he allows light, shadow, and negative space to participate in the construction of the image. Drawing thus becomes both an act of inscription and erasure: each incision reveals forms that appear to emerge directly from light.

His high-contrast compositions explore the relationship between presence and absence, visibility and concealment. A close observer of his surroundings, Galindo draws on memory, human experience, and everyday environments to suggest narratives that are not immediately visible. Rather than imposing a single interpretation, he creates contemplative images that invite viewers to complete the work through their own memories and associations.

Light is both a physical and conceptual material in his practice. It passes through or interacts with the incised surface, making absence visible and transforming emptiness into form. This process challenges conventional definitions of drawing while preserving its fundamental relationship with line, gesture, observation, and the construction of meaning.

Alongside his studio practice, Galindo has contributed to arts education in Barranquilla as a teacher at the Escuela Distrital de Artes y Tradiciones Populares. His work as an educator forms part of a broader commitment to drawing as a foundational artistic language and to the development of visual arts in the Colombian Caribbean.

In 2019, Galindo received the award in the Drawing – Established Artist category at the IV Salón Distrital de Artistas Plásticos y Visuales de Barranquilla. He was also selected for Imagen Regional 9, the national circulation and curatorial program organized by the Banco de la República.

His work has been presented in exhibitions and art platforms including Feria del Millón Caribe in Barranquilla; El dibujo es mentira II – La mano que miente at the Alliance Française in Barranquilla; Vacasquilla at the Gran Malecón; and La virtud de la luz at Elsa Piñeres Galería. In 2026, he participated in the third edition of Café Arte Musa, a collective exhibition presented by Musa Galería de Arte in Barranquilla.
