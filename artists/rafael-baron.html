Rafael Barón

Born Sahagún, Córdoba, ColombiaLives and works in Barranquilla, Colombia

Rafael Barón is a Colombian visual artist whose multidisciplinary practice brings together painting, photography, drawing, and mixed media. He studied Fine Arts at the Universidad del Atlántico in Barranquilla and Anthropology at the Universidad de Antioquia in Medellín, where he also pursued studies in photography, drawing, and pictorial techniques.

His work is rooted in figurative imagery and the graphic arts, constructing subjective spaces in which reality and fantasy continuously intersect. Through a deeply personal visual language, Barón explores memory, identity, nostalgia, and the transformation of lived experience into symbolic images.

Objects play a central role in his compositions. Toys, jewelry, animals, vegetation, clothing, places, and fragments of everyday life become carriers of memory and extensions of the lived body. His paintings often move between the recognizable and the dreamlike, inviting the viewer into intimate environments where personal recollection, imagination, and cultural references coexist.

This dialogue between the real and the fantastic gives his work a distinctive historical and melancholic dimension. Rather than treating technique as an end in itself, Barón uses materials, color, figures, and symbols to construct visual narratives that are intimate, honest, and deeply connected to memory.

His solo exhibitions include 1000 + 1 at Galería Otros 360 in Bogotá (2022), Bestiario Tropical at Casa Fundación Gases del Caribe in Barranquilla (2017), Plantación adentro at the Pinacoteca de la Aduana in Barranquilla (2016), and 0–142 metros sobre el nivel del mar at Centro Cultural Comfamiliar in Barranquilla (2014). He has also participated in numerous group exhibitions in Colombia and internationally.
