Patricia Quevedo

Born Barcelona, VenezuelaLives and works in Barranquilla, Colombia

Patricia Quevedo is a Colombian-Venezuelan visual artist whose practice spans painting, sculpture, and three-dimensional constructions. Developed over more than three decades, her work explores the relationships between geometry, color, rhythm, movement, and visual perception.

Quevedo’s visual language was shaped by her early encounters with Venezuelan kinetic art and her sustained interest in the work of Jesús Rafael Soto and Carlos Cruz-Diez. In her paintings and sculptural compositions, circles, squares, rectangles, triangles, lines, and grids are repeated, juxtaposed, and layered to create dynamic optical relationships. Contrasts between warm and cool colors make forms appear to advance, recede, expand, and contract.

Play is a central theme throughout her practice. Drawing on childhood memories, traditional games, puzzles, sports, and video games, Quevedo explores the challenges, emotions, strategies, and learning processes associated with play. Her compositions invite viewers to participate in the act of observation, deciphering internal systems and visual codes that are not immediately apparent.

Her recent work expands her geometric investigations into three-dimensional structures and new combinations of materials, including plexiglass, canvas, wood, acrylic, and direct acrylic printing. Through serial repetition and carefully balanced relationships between color, form, material, and space, she creates works that challenge perception while reflecting on memory, personal history, and the passage of time.

Quevedo has presented solo exhibitions in Colombia, Venezuela, and the United States. Her most recent solo exhibition, Pasadizos de la Historia, was presented at Galería Otros 360° in Bogotá in 2025. Other solo exhibitions include 3D at Galería de la Aduana in Barranquilla; El color toma forma at the Alliance Française in Barranquilla; Teoría, Práctica, Irrealidad at Galería Blasini in Caracas; and The Color of Black and White at Colombian Center Gallery in New York.

Her work has also been included in exhibitions and art fairs in Colombia, Venezuela, Panama, El Salvador, France, and the United States, including Circuito ARTBO, Art Miami, The Affordable Art Fair in New York, Saundarya at Galería Mmaison in Bogotá, Memorias, Reflexiones y Juegos at Galería 1-2-3 in San Salvador, and Art Capital at the Grand Palais in Paris.
