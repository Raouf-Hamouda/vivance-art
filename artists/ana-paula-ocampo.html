Ana Paula Ocampo

Born Reconquista, Santa Fe, ArgentinaLives and works in Reconquista, Argentina

Ana Paula Ocampo is an Argentine photographer, visual artist, graphic designer, and educator. She holds a degree in Graphic Design and Visual Communication from the Universidad Nacional del Litoral (UNL).

Her photographic practice explores memory, identity, and the poetic possibilities of the image, moving between intimate experience and collective narratives. With a sensitive and contemplative approach, Ocampo uses photography as a space where reality and poetry converge, inviting the viewer to reflect on human experience, cultural identity, and the traces left by memory.

Her work has been presented in individual and group exhibitions in Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Spain, Greece, France, Germany, and the United Arab Emirates. Among her projects is ORIGEN: voces ancestrales en las aulas, a black-and-white photographic series exploring intercultural education, diversity, and the transmission of ancestral knowledge.

Ocampo is also co-founder of SAUDADE, the Argentine Network of Photographers for Gestational and Perinatal Grief. Her photographs are held in public and private collections and have appeared on the covers of books and other publications.
