Framing and mounting

Every print can be framed and mounted to measure.

Frames come from a family-run, local and sustainable workshop known for the durability of its work. Ask for a quote when you acquire a piece.

Wood frame

A light wood frame that brings delicacy to the room. The wood comes from a family-run, local and sustainable framing workshop known for the durability of its products.

UV-protective acrylic frame

An acrylic frame with UV protection that gives a professional studio finish. Same local, family-run and sustainable workshop.

Full-bleed mounting

The print is framed flush to the edge, without a mat.

Mat mounting

The print sits on a 5 cm white mat with a bevel cut on every edge.

Float mounting

The print is raised so that both the front and the edges remain visible.
