Framing and mounting

Every work can be framed and mounted to measure, by a local workshop.

Five ways to frame a print or a work on paper. Ask for a quote when you acquire a piece, or write to us for a work you already own.

01 Wood frame02 UV-protective acrylic frame03 Full-bleed mounting04 Mat mounting05 Float mounting
01 / 05

Wood frame

A light wood frame that brings delicacy to the room. The wood comes from a family-run, local and sustainable framing workshop known for the durability of its products.

Material
Light natural wood
Best for
Paper, canvas, photography
Effect
Warm and soft, lets the work breathe
02 / 05

UV-protective acrylic frame

An acrylic frame with UV protection that gives a professional studio finish. Same local, family-run and sustainable workshop.

Material
Acrylic glazing with UV filter
Best for
Photography, works on paper, prints
Effect
Museum-grade protection against light
03 / 05

Full-bleed mounting

The print is framed flush to the edge, without a mat.

Material
No mat, image to the edge
Best for
Photography, bold graphic works
Effect
Clean, contemporary, maximum image
04 / 05

Mat mounting

The print sits on a 5 cm white mat with a bevel cut on every edge.

Material
5 cm white mat, bevel cut
Best for
Drawings, small formats, prints
Effect
Classic depth and breathing room
05 / 05

Float mounting

The print is raised so that both the front and the edges remain visible.

Material
Print raised on a hidden support
Best for
Deckled edges, handmade paper, textiles
Effect
Shows the edges, adds shadow and relief

How it works

1

Choose the work

In the shop or with us at the gallery. Every print and work on paper can be framed and mounted to measure.

2

We propose

Two or three frame and mounting options adapted to the work, the room and your budget, with a quote.

3

Made locally

Frames come from a family-run, local and sustainable workshop known for the durability of its work.

4

Delivered

Unique pieces are delivered personally, framed and ready to hang.
